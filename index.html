ABOUT IGNITE

A Celebration of Ideas, Innovation & Young Talent

IGNITE 2026 is the flagship science and innovation fest organized by the Department of Applied Science and Engineering, TKM Institute of Technology. Featuring a thrilling quiz competition and a creative project expo, IGNITE brings together young minds from schools to explore, compete, and showcase their potential.

Designed exclusively for students from High Schools (Class 8-10) and Higher Secondary Schools (+1 & +2), IGNITE provides an inspiring university campus exposure, hands-on scientific learning, peer interaction, and cash accolades for young visionaries.

✓
Prestigious Platform: Experience modern laboratories, advanced workshops, and campus life at TKMIT.
✓
Distinguished Jury: Project evaluation by esteemed professors and senior engineering innovators.
✓
Zero Registration Fees: Free entry for qualified school teams with lunch and participation kits included.
