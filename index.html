ABOUT IGNITE

A Celebration of Ideas, Innovation & Young Talent

IGNITE 2026 is the flagship science and innovation fest organized by the Department of Applied Science and Engineering, TKM Institute of Technology. Featuring a thrilling quiz competition, an inspiring project expo, and a dynamic reel making contest, IGNITE brings together young minds from schools to explore, compete, and showcase their potential.

Designed for students from High Schools and Higher Secondary Schools, IGNITE provides inspiring campus exposure, hands-on scientific learning, creative media expression, peer interaction, and cash accolades for young visionaries.

✓
Prestigious Platform: Experience modern laboratories, advanced workshops, and campus life at TKMIT.
✓
Distinguished Jury: Project evaluation by esteemed professors and senior engineering innovators.
✓
Zero Registration Fees: Free registration for all participating school teams.
